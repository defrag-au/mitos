⚙️ MEKKALABS INFRASTRUCTURE & ECOSYSTEM UPDATE 🐉

I want to give everyone a real update on where MekkaLabs is heading, what changes are happening behind the scenes, and why these decisions are being made.

After months of operating physical mining infrastructure, reviewing market conditions, testing Braiins Hashpower directly with live capital, and evaluating long-term sustainability, MekkaLabs will be transitioning away from operating the current 27 hosted ASIC miners and toward a managed hashrate deployment model using Braiins Hashpower.

This is not MekkaLabs stepping away from mining.

This is MekkaLabs evolving how mining is executed.

Why We Are Making This Change

The Bitcoin mining industry moves extremely fast.

Hardware cycles continue accelerating and new machine generations constantly reduce the long-term competitiveness of older ASIC fleets. Sitting passively in aging hardware while newer generations enter the market creates pressure on efficiency, resale value, maintenance exposure, and long-term treasury growth.

Instead of remaining locked into a traditional hardware-heavy model, MekkaLabs is moving toward a more flexible infrastructure strategy focused on:

capital efficiency
active treasury management
scalable deployment
preserving liquidity
reducing operational drag
maximizing reward retention

This gives MekkaLabs the ability to actively manage mining exposure rather than simply leaving machines running 24/7 regardless of market conditions.

Important Clarification

I want to make this very clear:

This is not a negative reflection on the hosting facility or the team operating the miners.

The machines were professionally managed and optimized using standard industry practices such as hashrate balancing, overclocking, underclocking, and uptime management.

This decision is simply about choosing the infrastructure model that works best for MekkaLabs moving forward.

We now control:

This transition significantly reduces several long-term operational risks:

no repair exposure
no hardware aging risk

Deployment Strategy

The deployment model will not be “all-in all the time.”

The strategy is layered.

MekkaLabs will maintain baseline mining exposure while actively scaling deployment during favorable hashprice conditions.

That means:
always maintaining mining exposure
dynamically managing bids
increasing deployment during favorable market conditions
preserving treasury efficiency during less favorable conditions

This approach creates flexibility while still maintaining consistent mining participation.
